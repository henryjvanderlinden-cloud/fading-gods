the levy marches

prototype · forty years · armies on the road

A levy no longer strikes from nowhere. It forms, and then it walks — one tile a year, and you can see exactly where it is going. You can wall it out by drowning the ground or raising mountains across its road, or you can send a bad omen and empty the town before it arrives, so that they take the walls and none of the people. But the people have to go somewhere, and where they go, they grow.
