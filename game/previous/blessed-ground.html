blessed ground

prototype · two powers · stones and cities

Where you have walked, the ground is blessed. Where a settlement has counted and ploughed it, the ground is reckoned. Standing on blessed ground with eight connected tiles beneath it, you can entice a sacred stone out of the earth. A stone is a conduit: it blesses the country around it every year without you, and once its region reaches sixteen it begins calling things down on settlements within reach. Blessed 3, reckoned 2, settlement 3.
