The seat control goes in the header, and the page says what the game is

Found by opening the build. The two-player switch was in the sidebar below
"on the road", the intervention list, the chart and "your stones", so on a
narrow window it sat below the whole board and the first person to look for
it could not find it. A control that changes what a year *is* should not be
below the fold, so the other seat and its two toggles move to the top right,
beside the title.

The title was still "the levy marches", which was the name of the build that
introduced marching armies and has been the name of the whole game ever since
by accident. It is Fading Gods, subtitled prototype.

The blurb underneath was about levies for the same reason - it described the
one feature that build had added rather than the game. Replaced with the
epigraph, a paragraph on what the game is, and a paragraph on how a year is
spent: three moves, one act, one intervention. Somebody opening this for the
first time now learns the ratchet - blessed ground, reckoned ground, and that
your own farmland walls you out - before they are asked to make a decision
about it.

No rules changed. smoke.js still passes 11,758 checks, including the two-seat
game played through the build's own buttons, and picking a doctrine back out
of the dropdown returns to a single-player game correctly.
