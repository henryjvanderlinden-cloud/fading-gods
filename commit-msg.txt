The terms go behind a door

OP-23, first of three. Everything on the page that was about the experiment
rather than about the valley now lives in one dialog reached from the header:
who is in the other seat, the hand-over and even-the-map toggles, which rule set
is being played, and the knobs.

One door and not two, though the seat block and the rules block looked like
separate concerns. They are the same category of object, and the code is what
says so - "even" restarts, every R2 checkbox restarts, both presets restart, the
knobs need "Restart with these", and choosing the human seat restarts. The only
control in there that does not is swapping one machine doctrine for another
mid-game, which is a debugging affordance rather than something a player does.
They are year-one controls. A year-one control beside the board is clutter with
a job title.

The header comes down with them: one line, the title on the left and the door on
the right, 18px of padding instead of 30.

<dialog> rather than an expander, because you are leaving the game to change the
terms of it and an expander would shove the board down the page. showModal()
gives the backdrop and Escape for free. jsdom does not implement <dialog>, so
openDlg falls back to the open attribute - smoke.js clicks #restart and counts
the eleven range inputs in #tune, and both are now inside one.

No rules changed. smoke.js passes 11,758 checks, the same count as before,
including the full game and the two-seat game played through the build's own
buttons.
