OP-23: the interface is a laboratory with a game in it

Raised from reading the build rather than playing it. The page interleaves two
documents that have nothing to do with each other - the valley, and the
instrument being used on it. A slider reading "growth per year 22%" eight pixels
under the chronicle does more damage to the register than any colour decision in
OP-17.

The split is not taste, and the code says so: almost every control in the
instrument half restarts the game when touched. They are year-one controls and
they belong behind one door.

The entry also records the regression it takes knowingly - 7623cee put the
primer on the page on purpose, and nothing else on the page teaches the ratchet -
and it does the tablet arithmetic the register had been carrying as an assertion.
An 11-inch iPad is 1194 x 834 in landscape, which is the orientation this is
meant to be played in. Two things fall out. The stacking breakpoint at 1180
misses that device by fourteen points and leaves it drawing a 792px board, hexes
at 31 for art authored at 50. And height, not width, is the binding constraint:
745 points of viewport, 650 of them board, 95 left for the 275 the chrome needs.
The board has to give.

No rules changed, and nothing is built yet. Three commits follow.
