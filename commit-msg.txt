OP-25, and the herds are ruled on

Two things, and the second is the larger.

OP-24 is decided. Rick: they become fully autonomous, it is a one-way
act, they may become too large and split at a given size, and being out
of hearing is not a problem - he thinks they should be. So all three
questions the entry opened are answered, and answered harder than it
proposed. The audibleHerd collision stops being a collision and becomes
the design: a band past the Seventy-Seven has walked out of hearing and
stops adding courses to stones, which is a sentence rather than a bug.
The logistic stops being a ceiling and becomes the pressure that drives
the split, so the fuse gets set from the settles-at column and not from
two Seventy-Sevens. Two consequences recorded before they are
discovered: kurgans lose their only trigger and need another or need
cutting, and the detour framing in 1.19 is now false - herding is a door
after all, kept honest by scoring nothing rather than by coming back.

OP-25 is new and sits beside OP-21 rather than below it. OP-21 is the
instrument; this is the question. Rick's, and raised as the thing he is
deliberately not deciding yet: creation is the first act, the middle has
become interdiction through agriculture and through herds, and what the
late game rewards is open.

The engine answers more bluntly than expected. The valley is 84 walkable
tiles: 252 points blessed, 168 ploughed. Total points on the board peak
at year twelve or thirteen at about 230 of a possible 252 - 91% of
everything that can ever exist - and fall for the remaining 27 years.
Two settled powers destroy 29% of everything they ever made. So as the
build stands, the late game is interdiction only by arithmetic: there is
nothing left to reward because there is nothing left to make.

Five directions written down, one measurement each where one was cheap.
Integrating over time is computable today, since FG.G.hist has recorded
the full component score every year since the beginning and nothing
reads it but the chart: win rates go cities 42 to 29, haunt 55 to 79,
bands 33 to 46. Read that as what the present game looks like under a
different lens, not as what the game would become - every DOCTRINE
weight is tuned to the current score, so measuring a new scoring against
this chooser measures the chooser. OP-01 in its purest form.

The order is part of the ruling and is written down: gameplay first,
scoring after, and no scoring change may be made to fix a balance
problem. Build sim/rescore.js first because it is nearly free and
answers OP-20's standing ask at the same time. Then stop, and play.
