The chip row gets its own checks, and the register says what a point is

Seventeen checks fold into sim/smoke.js, driven through the build's own DOM the
way the rest of that section is. 11,758 -> 11,775.

What they are actually guarding is not the interventions but the row. Everything
keeps its place in it - a lost wonder struck through, a work that has not opened
dim - because the row emptying from the left and filling from the right is what
tells the arc. A later tidy-up that started dropping chips it thought were
useless would look perfectly fine on screen and would have thrown that away. So:
the row is six wide at year forty whatever has been lost, every lost wonder is
still in it with the gone class, and the struck-out ones are the first lostCount
of them rather than an arbitrary set.

The rest cover the things the refactor invented. A locked work says what strength
it needs and explains itself when pressed instead of arming - it is off by class
and not by the disabled attribute, precisely so that it can. Arming marks exactly
one chip and puts it down again. The armed hint carries the description as well
as the target instruction, which is the whole reason the chips are readable on a
tablet with no hover. Hover borrows the hint line and gives it back. The
teachings appear and vanish with FG.R2.teaching, and the group label with them.

Verified by breaking it: removing the gone class from the chip template fails two
of the new checks and nothing else, so they are not vacuous.

And a note on units in OP-23, because the register had been writing "points"
while the stylesheet writes px. On this page they are the same number - the head
sets width=device-width, initial-scale=1, so an 11-inch iPad reports innerWidth
1194 - and OP-21's figure was right. But CSS pt is a different unit, 1.333px, and
tidying the board cap's 340px to 340pt would quietly cost the board a hundred and
forty-five pixels. Written down so nobody does it. OP-21's tablet bullet stops
saying "deferred deliberately" and points here.

No rules changed.
