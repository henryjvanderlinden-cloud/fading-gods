A third leg, stones that grow, and a turn that is a generation

Three ideas, none of them measured, all of them registered rather than built.

The pastoralists are the third leg OP-12 has been missing since the beginning,
and they cost less than they look: a third entry in FG.TEACH beside till and
kill, inheriting everything OP-19 already built for teaching. Taught only to a
people who never learned the plough, and only once somebody somewhere has
ploughed - a condition rather than a clock, so A-10 stays intact. They hold no
ground and score nothing while they roam. What they do is graze reckoned ground
back, which is Wither the furrows performed by mortals instead of by you.

Two things go in with them. The loop's third row - what stops a host, the
question wandering hosts never answered - is the other power's blessing only,
because blessing impassable to herds walls your own herds in with your own
blessing. And the kurgans, which are the better half of the idea: a mound raised
over a stone that has already gone under farmland, with no reach, no blessing
and nothing targeting through it, counting only as a monument standing. OP-16
measured that 92% of stones end that way and that it cannot be reversed. Kurgans
do not reverse it. They make it mean something, which is a better answer than the
one that register went looking for.

Recorded with its failure mode, because it is a real one: herding is a third door
out of the central dilemma, and if a player can expand without ever teaching the
plough then the trade the whole game is built on can simply be declined.

Stones that grow go into ideas.md with the argument that keeps them out of
rejected.md. A stone growing on an age counter is a timer and A-10 kills it. A
stone that grows because there are people near it who still hear you is a stock
you built and can lose - so the course goes on while a settlement under
seventy-seven stands in reach, and stops the year you teach that band to till.

OP-22 asks whether turns are years or generations. Mostly a label, and the parts
that are not are gains: the demography stops being nonsense, and it is the unit
the other two ideas are denominated in. The one real cost is marching, and the
only question in the entry is what a levy becomes at twenty-five years a tile.

OP-12 goes from low to medium and is blocked on OP-19 - a third leg laid on an
untuned second leg is the trap OP-19 already names. OP-01 records the AI as
deliberately deferred: an instrument built against a rule set that is still
moving would have to be built again. Storm & Sky stop being indifferent in
lore.md and take the third answer, which is the position pastoralism actually is.

No rules changed. Nothing in engine/, game/ or sim/ was touched.
