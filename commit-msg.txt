Ideas: what the world does without you, measured

Rick: what if after 40 turns settlements that can raise a levy sometimes do it on
their own? The right instinct, and moveColumns already resets a captured
settlement's ploughing budget, so conquest looks like it should hand a farming
power thirty fresh tiles.

Built and run at 2%, 5% and 15% a generation. The wars happen and the ground does
not move: towns change hands — storm 5.6 to 4.9, cities 5.5 to 6.2 — and blessing
reads 28.8, 28.9, 29.1 across every rate, identical to no wars at all.

Because the gate is not the budget, it is `taught`. Only a people shown the plough
turn ground into fields, and a levy that takes a town does not teach it. Conquest
moves ownership and never moves agriculture.

Tested the one-line version — a place taken by levy takes the teaching of whoever
took it, which pairs with encirclement forbidding both teachings for ever:

                            gen 80   gen 240   taught towns
  conquest does not teach     28.9      29.1     2.8 -> 2.8
  conquest teaches            27.6      26.2     2.8 -> 4.0

It moves, at 2.7 tiles of blessing per two hundred generations. A seepage, not a
takeover.

So the finding is bigger than the ending. Every dynamic in this game is powered by
a god, and the valley is in equilibrium at year forty by construction — ploughing
is a lifetime allowance that is spent, ploughing requires `taught` and teaching is
an act of a god, and blessing is only made by a god or an audible settlement. The
valley is only alive while somebody is watching it.

Which may be the theme, but it makes extrapolation impossible and the assessment
trivial: on a frozen board everyone with one blessed tile is remembered for ever.
The ending is therefore not a scoring question. It is: what does the world do
without you, and that has to be answered first.

Rick's three intuitions against the engine as it stands: herds keep their
mythology, already true and nobody built it; blessing maintains if walled off,
true and far too true — haunt is 34.4 blessed tiles at generation 40 and 34.4 at
240, mathematically permanent; agriculture takes over by weight of numbers, false
twice over and the one that needs a rule.
