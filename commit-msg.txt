The interventions come out from beside the board and go under it

OP-23, last of three. The 400px column of eleven named interventions, each with
its description and its own button, becomes a row of chips directly beneath the
act row.

The size was the smaller of the two problems. design/rules.md §2 marks one act
and one intervention as separate slots and load-bearing - they are the two halves
of a year - and the two halves were three hundred pixels apart, in different
type, under different headings. They are adjacent now.

Nothing is removed from the row. A lost wonder keeps its place struck through, a
work that has not opened keeps its place dim. That is the point rather than a
concession: gone = i < lostN, so wonders die from the front of FG.DIVINE while
works open in ladder order, and the row therefore empties from the left and fills
from the right. Over forty turns it turns from blessing-green to farmland-ochre
along a single horizontal axis, which is the arc of the whole game as a progress
bar, narrated by nothing. The column could not do that.

The descriptions had nowhere to go and tooltips do not work on a tablet, so they
go into the hint line, which was already sitting under the act row saying "choose
an outlined tile". Hover or keyboard-focus a chip and the line describes it; arm
one and the description arrives beside the target instruction, before anything
has been spent. Arming was always two-step, so the confirm step was there all
along and no tooltip was ever needed.

Chips are off by class and not by the disabled attribute. A disabled button fires
no pointer events, and a chip you cannot use still has to answer when you press
it - "not yet, this needs strength 9", "nothing in reach", "gone, and it does not
come back" - rather than refusing in silence.

And the tablet arithmetic, measured in a browser at 1680x1050, 1440x900 and
1194x745 rather than argued. The stacking breakpoint moves 1180 -> 1280, which it
had to: 1180 caught a 10.9-inch Air exactly and missed an 11-inch Pro by fourteen
points, so that device kept two columns and drew a 792px board, hexes at 31 for
art drawn at 50. The playing column is now capped by the height available rather
than the width - min(1272, max(880, (100vh - 340px) x 1.775)) - and the 880 floor
is deliberate, because without it the arithmetic runs away: a narrower board
wraps the chip row onto more lines, which demands a narrower board.

What that buys, honestly: on an 11-inch iPad in landscape the board is 880 wide,
hex 35 against 31, with the bar, the board and the act row above the fold and the
chip row about 60 points below it. Everything fits on a laptop and above. A 14x9
board at a readable size plus two control rows does not fit in 745 points and no
layout makes it.

No rules changed. smoke.js passes 11,758 checks, and a separate pass over the
chips - twenty checks - confirms the six and three stay six and three, that a
locked chip explains instead of arming, that hover borrows the hint line and
gives it back, that the teachings appear and vanish with FG.R2.teaching, and that
by year forty the struck-out wonders are exactly the first lostCount of them.
