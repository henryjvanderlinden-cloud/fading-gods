The owner-blind grazing, measured, and left alone

Grazing has never asked whose furrow it is. That is 1.19's behaviour and it was
left through both of yesterday's commits because Rick ruled on where the bands
aim and never on what they eat. Wandering made it a live question — a band
waiting out the early years drifts through your own country — so it is measured
rather than argued about.

  their furrows eaten          18.11 a game
  YOUR furrows eaten            0.16 a game — 0.9% of everything they graze
  games where it happens         6.2%
  when                          43 of 50 times while walking at a target,
                                not while waiting
  median year                   38

And making them refuse, paired over the same 322 games: win +0.31 ± 0.61, your
score +0.13 ± 0.14, their score 0.00 ± 0.16. Only your own farmland count moves
— +0.09 tiles ± 0.06, significant and meaningless.

So it stays as it is. Not because the harder reading is better here but because
the question is empty: one game in sixteen, at the end of a long game, to a band
that was walking somewhere else. A special case that buys nothing should not be
written.

The same run corrects every per-game figure in A-33, all of which were measuring
bands dying in the year they were taught:

                 defect   corrected
  bands            1.4      4.9
  band-years      13.5     55.2     (7.9 of them spent waiting)
  tiles grazed     6.3     18.3
  people taken     112      443
  splits           0.3      0.5
  settlings        0.6      0.3
  mounds           0.4      0.1

Four times the rule for one line of code, and the door back onto the plough is
half as common as it was — which is right, because only the adversary opens it
now. No engine change in this commit.
