A-33, and the third leg does not pay for itself

OP-24 is settled and archived. A-33 carries the eleven rulings, the two things
the code said that the register did not, and the measurement — 900 seeds, the
322 in which a band ever walked, both arms on the same set:

                  win        score      their farmland   their big towns
  steered (1.19)  54.3% ±2.8  82.0:76.1      13.5             5.0
  roaming (1.24)  48.4% ±2.8  73.2:74.1      13.8             5.3

Autonomy costs the side that uses it six points of win rate and nine of score,
and denies the settled side nothing. Their farmland does not fall. Their big
towns do not fall. The passive denial engine A-32 said the magical side did not
have, it still does not have — and that absence is the finding, because it was
the whole argument for building the rule.

The direction of the cost is 1.5 sigma and not proven. The absence of the denial
is not a difference of means and does not need to be.

So OP-26 opens, as a rule question and not a scoring one. Two candidate causes,
both testable in an afternoon and both written down rather than guessed at: a
band that settles comes home taught and costs a wonder, where stopping one used
to be three free points; and one tile a year against a side that re-ploughs may
simply be competing with the ratchet and losing. The order of the three
measurements is in the entry, and the last of them is a played game.

Two things the entry is explicit that nobody may do: change scoring to fix it
(OP-25, and the ninth rule of the method), and re-weight `storm` to make the rule
look better — every number here comes from one chooser with `herd: 1`, which is
OP-01's warning stated as a measurement.
