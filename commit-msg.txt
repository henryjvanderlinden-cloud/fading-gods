Two dead rules, and a check that was counting something unbounded

Housekeeping found by looking rather than by playing.

button.arm and button.armc are gone. They coloured the arm buttons in the
intervention column, which no longer exists - the chips carry their own state in
.chip.on. .hintline.arm is a different selector and stays.

And the new chip check failed once in five runs, which was worth chasing rather
than re-running. Two things behind it, both real.

lostCount() is settlements past the threshold less working stones, and nothing
clamps it to six. Once it passes six the row saturates - only six chips exist to
strike through - and the check was comparing the struck count against a number
that can be seven. The game is right either way, because divineLeft() slices, but
the assertion was wrong and is now against min(lostCount, DIVINE.length), with
the reason written next to it.

The second is older and is not mine. newGame() does not pass a seed, and the
engine section at the top of smoke.js seeds a different FG instance, so every
game this file has ever played through the interface ran on Math.random. Fine for
"does it play at all", which is what that section was, and not fine the moment
anything counts. The counting part now pins the seed. Three runs, 11,775 checks,
identical.
