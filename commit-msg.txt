A band with nowhere to go waits, and A-33 was measuring a defect

Rick played it: a band he had just taught became a settlement again in the same
year. His guess at why was right and it was mine.

Two defects, both introduced yesterday, compounding. `ploughed()` asks whether
the board has a furrow on it and not whose, so an agricultural arm of your own
unlocks herding years before the adversary has broken ground — which is fine, and
was never the problem. The problem is that the second rank of `herdAim` is very
nearly unreachable: *the open ground beside a town of theirs* sounds like a
fallback and is not one, because a town stands in its own blessing and every tile
touching it is closed country to a band. So a band born early had nothing in
either rank, 1.24 read that as boxed in, and it handed away a town and a wonder in
the year it was taught.

Rick's ruling: *let them wander through your own blessed lands, until the opponent
unlocks agriculture, then the wayfinding points to the agricultural land.* So
nothing to walk at is waiting, not ending. `herdWander` drifts them through their
own god's country — never closed to them — on `FG.rand` so the seeds still hold.
A people who cannot take a single step, ringed on every side by the other power's
quiet, are the only ones who stop. The door back onto the plough is now opened by
the adversary and by nobody else, at a price in acts, rather than opening by
itself for a band that was early.

And it overturns yesterday's table. Same 322 games, paired seed by seed, roaming
minus steered, 95% intervals:

  win rate         +2.8  ± 5.1   not significant
  your score       +0.6  ± 2.1   not significant — it costs its user nothing
  their score      -4.0  ± 1.7   significant
  their farmland   -2.5  ± 0.5   significant

The passive denial engine is real and it is measured. A-32 said the magical side
had no answer to a plough that erases blessing for free; this is the answer — two
and a half tiles a game that never get sown, and four points off the settled side,
at no cost to the side that sent the people out. The win rate does not move and
should not be expected to: four points is small against the spread of a forty-year
game. The mechanism was the question.

The fingerprint says it in one game. Seed 0 read 49:122 under the first cut
against the steered 90:71; wandering restores it to 90:69. The two older arrays
did not move at all, again.

OP-26 is withdrawn an hour after it was raised. It was a real question about a
number that was measuring a bug, and the seventh rule of the method read
backwards: the null was not a null, it was a rule killing its own instrument
before it could act, and the only reason it was caught is that Rick played a game.

Checks: 11,976, all passing. Kurgans got worse — 0.4 a game down to 0.1, because a
wandering band crosses its own silent stones by luck rather than by walking at
them. And grazing is still owner-blind, which was theoretical while they only ever
walked at the adversary and is not any more: a band waiting out the early years
drifts through your country and will eat your own furrows if it crosses them.
